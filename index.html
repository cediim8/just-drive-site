Questions

What is a car logbook for tax?

To claim work-related car expenses with the ATO's logbook method, you keep a logbook of your driving for at least 12 continuous weeks that's representative of your travel. It gives your work-use percentage, and a logbook stays valid for 5 years unless your circumstances change. The ATO explains the logbook method here.

Does Just Drive meet the ATO's logbook requirements?

It keeps records to ATO logbook requirements: the purpose and destination of each work trip, odometer readings at the start and end of each trip and of the 12 weeks, and the kilometres travelled. It's a record-keeping app, not tax advice; your accountant decides what you claim.

Does it really log trips by itself?

Yes. It uses your phone's location and motion sensors to notice when you start driving, records the trip, and saves it once you've parked. You can also choose to start each trip yourself instead.

What about my battery and privacy?

Location is used when you're driving, not all day. There's no account and no Just Drive server: your trips stay on your phone and in your own backup.

How much does it cost?

Logging and sorting are free. When your 12 weeks are done, a Logbook Pass unlocks the finished logbook for that car: A$29.99, once. There's no subscription.
